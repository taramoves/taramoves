What this is

Working notes towards a literature review on practice-based research in fulldome performance — the spectrum from theatrical work in a dome, through the performance lecture, to the actual lecture. They are kept as pages rather than one document because the map, the examples and the bibliography are read differently.

The notes are built from a 45-source corpus held locally as PDFs. Quotations are machine-checked against the extracted source text and the page they are attributed to before anything is written down; where a scan is damaged, the entry says so instead of guessing.

Reading route

Examples first — the works, on the theatrical→lecture axis. Then Threads for the three lines of enquiry. Then Bibliography for what each source actually argues. The Map is for when the question is what connects to what.

This is an unlisted working site: the pages are reachable by URL, are not in the site menu, and are marked noindex,nofollow so they do not turn up in search results.
