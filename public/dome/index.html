D-01Reading notes — the literature review live · unlisted · noindex

Kept as pages rather than one document, because the map, the examples and the bibliography are read differently. Built from a 45-source corpus held locally as PDFs.

pages
Examples (3,361 words) · Threads (3,068) · Bibliography (93,180) · Sources wanted (3,683) · Leads (1,168) · Map (27,432)
corpus
45 sources, held locally; 325+ quoted passages
provenance
Every quotation is machine-checked against the extracted source text and the page it is attributed to before it is written down. Where a scan is damaged the entry says so instead of guessing. Where a work is not held, the claim states its provenance and nothing is quoted from it.
map
The wiki behind the notes as a graph, a source table, a matrix and indexes, with coverage filters — which sources touch which ideas, and where the gaps are.
leads
Works the review needs and the corpus does not hold: what the corpus already says about them and what would change if they arrived. Schawinsky's Spectodrama first.

D-02ESCARGOT — technology outline live · working draft, September 2026

The project has a technological part and a narrative part. This document covers only the technology. The phone runs a web app; a hub — a server at the venue — receives everything the phones send, decides what it means, logs it, and drives the dome's visuals and spatial audio.

sections
1 Overview · 2 Calibrate · 3 Interactions · 4 Facilitator · 5 Hub · 6 Plan
question it answers
What a phone knows on its own, the camera picture that gives it heading and seat, and how a calibrated phone puts a cursor on the dome; the objects, the five things a phone can do, and what the dome makes of each; how a scene is defined and a show is sequenced; messages, output and logging; then work packages in build order with deliverables and open questions.
form
One page, 423 KB, with the mathematics set in LaTeX. No build step.

D-03Fulldome festival database live · unlisted

Screening, credit and award records assembled from printed programmes and catalogues, one row per screening, reconciled across festivals so a film that travelled is one film rather than six programme entries.

festivals
7 — Best of Earth, Dome Fest West, Dome Under, Fulldome Festival Brno, Fulldome Festival Jena, Fulldome UK, SAT Fest
scale
53 editions, 2010–2026 · 1,662 screenings · 1,202 films · 1,756 artists · 102 countries of origin
awards
329 screenings carry an award status (winner, nominee, honourable mention, finalist, commendation, special mention); 260 films hold at least one
descriptions
913 rows carry a synopsis, with a confidence line — high or medium — and its source
provenance
All 1,662 rows carry their source file, the page or poster, and an evidence quote. A 47-value flag vocabulary records what the source did not print and how the row was obtained — country not printed (626 rows), director not printed (354), transcribed from programme poster image (74) — so a gap is visible rather than silent.
use
Search and filters across every field; five views — Screens, Films, Artists, Editions, Awards; CSV export; shareable filtered links.

D-04Dome work analysis — the eval model on disk · not published

One record per authored work made for a domed projection space — film, performance, lecture, interactive or generative work, hybrids. Framework v2. AURA (Katrina Lat, 2025) is the evaluation spine, used as published; everything added here is marked. A v1 audit cut the record from 107 fields to 70, of which 48 are the working CORE view.

design rules
Describe first, evaluate later — no value language before §5. No aggregate score, and marks are never summed or totalled anywhere in the interface or the exports. Dome-native vocabulary throughout: dome master, equidistant azimuthal fisheye, concentric/unidirectional seating, 15.1, the springline. Unknown stays unknown, kept distinct from not applicable.
held as
FRAMEWORK.md — the specification, v2 (18.6 KB) · RUBRIC.md — the record sheet, generated from the tool so paper cannot drift from app · dome-work-analysis.html — the tool, one offline file (94 KB) · blank-form.pdf — the empty rubric, 10 pages · example-analysis-record.pdf — a filled record, 11 pages
tool
Three views: an Index of every work as a sortable row; an Analysis view carrying the dome profile, the provenance chain, a chapter timeline, a dome occupancy map with the seat line, an unrolled dome surface, the computed profile and the pillar marks; and a Blank form. Records persist in the browser's local storage, with JSON export and import, and each work exports as markdown.
computed
Spatial footprint, seat-band coverage, zenith time and dome occupancy, derived from the chapters rather than typed in.
status
Not published. No URL yet — this entry is a placeholder until it is put up.
