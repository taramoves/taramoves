Onwards and upwards. Unmask, unwind, untether. Dance as the sun and the moon go by.

Flicker and Gambol features a spatial audio mix of Steinman's 2024 song "heal, my love," abstracting cycles of trauma and healing into colour, image, and dance. It has toured internationally, including at Montreal's own SATFest, Australia's Dome Under festival, Uruguay's Montevideo festival, and Germany's FullDome Festival Jena.

Made as part of **MANYBIRDS**, the audiovisual collaboration between Tara Rose Morris and composer Graham Steinman.
