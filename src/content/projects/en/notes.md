A one-minute animation that reimagines two years of illustrated journal entries into an amalgamated artist's manifesto. Acrylic-marker drawings, loosely inspired by Indian craft painting and Lithuanian folk art, flicker across the screen as collaged aphorisms and visualizations. The work reframes private notations as public proposition, inviting viewers to witness the artist thinking out loud. Also imagined for immersive installation.
