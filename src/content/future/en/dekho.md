500 years ago, the DEKHO project planted its seedling communities across the galaxy.

Diasporic outposts, villages, and cities emerged with customs, traditions, practices, and systems that echoed their ancestors and imagined new liberatory futures.

Now we travel as an archeologist, documenting these early worlds and understanding our own journey along the way.

Each planet is an audiovisual collaboration between South Asian artists.

As film, performance, and interactive experience, each world offers a quest along the way to discovery:

- follow the path to color
- perform a ritual at the shrine
- observe the world around you
