DOMEBOOK is a beautiful, fun-to-read textbook of sorts on the philosophy and practice of fulldome as a new media art form.

It is written primarily for traditional animators and creative technologists who are not yet working in fulldome, with the aim of getting them to try the medium.

**Covering:**

- history & theory of dome
- spatial & temporal design principles
- technical approaches to development
- pedagogical exercises

**References include:** Paul Klee's *Pedagogical Sketchbook*, the *Whole Earth Catalog*'s *Domebook*, the *Journal of the International Planetarium Society*, Micky Remann and Kelly Francis's *Towards a Fulldome Manifesto*, Cora Braun's *Typology of Dome*, Mourad Bennacer and Vincent Brault's *Fulldome Immersive Creation: A Panorama*, and much more.

Supported by surveys of contemporary fulldome artists, curators, and technicians.
