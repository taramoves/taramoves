What possibilities emerge when ancient practice is placed in conversation with modern computation? What is noticed, and what is missed?

Bharatanatyam is an ancient Indian dance form ruled by algorithmic principles that dictate poses, movement, sound, and narrative.

Using live pose tracking with a specialty pose classification model, TouchDesigner, ossia score, webcam, and projector, we will experiment with:

- expressive subtitling for audiences
- generative scoring for dancers

In both directions, we hope to examine the distortions that emerge.

NRITTA.EXE is a collaborative research project by choreographer Rachana Joshi and creative technologist Tara Rose Morris.
