Not all domes are built for dancing.

But this one is.

So how do we get everyone dancing together?

MOVES creates an environment from participation that encourages participation. Each dance move is recorded photobooth-style and placed into generative visuals, accompanied by music that makes you move.

Anonymity is preserved while retaining identity. Surveillance technologies are retooled for collective joy.

- See yourself and your friends in the visuals
- Never run out of dance moves
- Shake ass till the sun comes up
