The power of the immersive format depends on the clarity of the relationship between spatial audio and immersive image.

**Idea:** create interfaces — digital and physical — that let artists and audiences play the dome like an instrument. Those interfaces should be intuitive and generous. Points of inspiration include Marie LeBlanc Flanagan's play under the dome and Lucas Paris's dome performance.

For every interactive sketch, participants should be able to understand precisely what is happening and have the power to act on how events unfold. HOPSCOTCH could stand alone as an installation, or appear before / between shows.

**Back to basics:** spatial audio <> immersive visual — simple, legible to audiences, and captivating.
